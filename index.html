Contour

The land moves while your agent works. A pin marks it done.

A survey field-sheet theme for T3 Code. Warm paper, sienna contours and one survey-orange for things that need you. During a turn the contours in the margins shift elevation; when it ends, a survey pin is driven into the summit and everything holds still.

Live turn · 1:1 Contour interval 50 m 1 2 3 4 5 6
  1. Shifting contours. Three elevations of the same terrain, each held for about three seconds. The gutters dim a little, step one level and come back, so labels never blur into each other. Streams and marks stay put.
  2. Streams run downhill. They are traced by steepest descent from the same height field, so they cross the lines at right angles.
  3. Survey stake. A stake walks an elevation profile along the header for the length of the turn.
  4. Theodolite. The pin lifts away and an orange reticle marks the summit.
  5. Working row. The live row gets hachure ticks, the survey mark for a hollow.
  6. Legend. A slim scale strip, docked low in the sidebar above Settings, clear of the thread list. On narrow windows it becomes a small map strip with the pin.
Sheet 02 · What moves, and why

If nothing is running, nothing moves.

Every animation is keyed to real app state. Idle is a still map with a pin in it. That is on purpose: you read all day, and a calm map is the right background for it.

StateWhat you seeWhy
Idle

A still sheet, pin planted

Contours, labels, streams and a pin on the summit. Nothing animates.

Turn running

The contours shift elevation

Ping-pong through three terrain frames on a 15 second cycle. Each is held for about three seconds; the contour layer dims to 60%, steps and returns, while streams, marks and names stay still. After two cycles (30 seconds) it settles: one elevation held, breathing slowly, so a long turn stays calm.

Turn running

Reticle and walking stake

The instrument takes over from the pin: a reticle marks the summit and a stake walks the header's elevation profile.

Thread busy

A survey line sweeps the row

Any thread with an agent on it gets an orange line sweeping along the foot of its row, clear of the title and time. You can see background work at a glance.

Turn complete

The pin drops

A survey pin falls onto the summit, bounces and lands, with a ripple. It plays when a turn ends, never on load. Switching away from a running thread can trigger it too. It is the one flourish, and it means "done".

Hover

Contour rings on the row

A few static rings fade in from the right edge of a thread row, as if the pointer were a hill.

Composer focus

The neatline lights up

The composer is framed like a map neatline; focus turns the outer line survey-orange.

Narrow window

The scale strip becomes a map strip

Below 1460px the gutters are thin, so the margins keep soft, label-free contour lines and the sidebar foot shows a small map strip: pin planted at rest, reticle while a turn runs.

With prefers-reduced-motion, every animation and transition stops and the sheet shows its idle still, in both the theme and this page.

Sheet 04 · Palette

Paper, ink, one orange.

Paper is the field sheet and ink is the pencil. Orange is the only loud colour, and it is saved for focus, send and work in progress. Click a row to copy its hex.

Every value here is read straight from contour.json at build time. The sienna contours, hydro-blue water and sage stripe are drawn in the art, so they are not roles.

Sheet 05 · Install

Two ways in.

From the app

  1. Copy the theme JSON, or download contour.json.
  2. In T3 Code open Settings › Appearance › Add theme.
  3. Paste the JSON, or choose the file, and add it.
Download

From the terminal

curl -fsSL -o contour.json https://joshsaintjacque.github.io/t3code-theme-contour/contour.json
~/.t3/bin/t3 theme set contour.json

That publishes the file and sets it as the environment theme, so every client on that environment switches. Tested on T3 Code 0.1.11 (nightly).
